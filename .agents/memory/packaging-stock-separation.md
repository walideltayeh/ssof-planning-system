---
name: Packaging stock separation
description: Business constraint for Old/New packaging analysis and clearance estimates
---

Old and New are managed as separate stock. Keep their sales, stock and cover separate even for the same flavour and weight; never use New sales to clear Old stock.

**Why:** The user explicitly stated that Old and New are managed as separate stock, and the team needs to distinguish them throughout Analysis.

**How to apply:** SKU labels must identify flavour/name, weight and packaging. Complete clearance must respect each SKU's own demand rather than assume stock can move between packaging types or flavours. Aggregate stock/rate duration is not proof that every SKU will clear.
